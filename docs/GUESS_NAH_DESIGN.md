# Guess Nah design gate

Updated: 2026-10-02. Status: researched proposal; recognition testing and human playtesting pending.

## Owner direction

The owner is willing to restart Guess Nah's data structure. The cricket-heavy experience is not sufficiently enjoyable; meaningful deduction and recognisable people take priority over preserving the old taxonomy. Design the roster and rules before rewriting Guess scoring, admin tools or schema. This does not authorize an immediate production wipe. Keep Where Nah's separate coverage/reliability work and the urgent security fixes on the project roadmap.

## Proposed beta

Nominate 32 people with only two cricketers and at most three sharing a primary speciality. Public figures must be T&T-born or have a substantial documented T&T career; preserve the actual connection rather than assign every icon a Trinidad birthplace. Recognition testing decides admission. Do not fill quotas with obscure names.

The live snapshot had seven cricketers, 11 sports-tagged people and no women among 12. The draft has nine women and meaningful music/sport/creator/public-life/heritage variety. These are catalogue proportions, not evidence of popular recognition.

Primary lane is editorial. Graded Known for sets may include a second substantial career: Heather Headley Music/Screen and stage, Ato Boldon Sport/Broadcast, Errol Fabien Broadcast/Stage comedy. Source these before approval; do not grade a genuine major career as unrelated merely to simplify the grid.

| Proposed column | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Known for | Controlled career sets: same set exact, genuine overlap partial, none wrong |
| Speciality | Controlled activity/genre sets with the same exact/overlap rule |
| Born | Confirmed year: equal exact, answer born earlier/later directional, within five years also near |
| Gender | Publicly documented/self-described, exact/different/unknown; never infer from a picture |

Unknown on either side is neutral, never a green match or a red factual contradiction. Display the submitted person's year to make the arrow usable. Do not use schools, ethnicity, religion, current follower counts, reach/popularity or current office as casual daily discriminators. Do not add an artificial unique tag to fix a collision.

Test eight attempts, canonical duplicate rejection, and free automatic hints after 3/5/7 misses: primary lane, sourced work/achievement/brand, then portrait or strong text identity clue. The fifth-miss clue should leave at most three compatible people. Recognition and accessibility matter; timed hints must be equal for all ranked players and must not be sold for currency.

Practice is unranked/replayable. A daily freezes roster/profile/rules/hints and uses America/Port_of_Spain business dates. Do not assume a source for a biographical fact also permits reuse of its portrait.

## Evidence and open facts

Official/publisher research covered Wordle/Worldle, LoLdle, Rivalsdle, Actorle, Spotle, Poeltl, Who Are Ya?, Weddle and Bandle. Their useful patterns are finite editions, scoped pools, real partial membership, directional numeric feedback and progressive recognition clues. This was public rules/source review, not a recorded playtest of every site. Some client pages exposed little text.

An offline draft model assumed complete roster knowledge and perfect elimination, with no hints or human recognition errors:

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Four columns | 2.469 | 3 | 3.002 |
| Remove Born | 3.500 | 6 | 4.115 |
| Remove Speciality | 2.531 | 4 | 3.046 |
| Remove Gender | 2.688 | 4 | 3.292 |

Current live 12 with the existing eight columns: optimal mean 2.000, maximum 3. The legacy replay matched the actual shared comparator on all 132 nonidentity ordered pairs. That does not establish the current game is fun: schools/teams can be informative to a bot and unhelpful to ordinary players.

A wrong opening can still leave 20 draft candidates. Do not promise every guess gives perfect direction. The finite budget and reviewed clue ladder need human validation.

Six years remain deliberately unconfirmed: Kees Dieffenthaller, Learie Joseph, Certified Sampson, Ro’dey, Ian Alleyne and Beryl McBurnie. The draft flags the identical Sampson/Ro’dey categorical profile with unknown years. Author/clear discriminating clues before admitting them to a ranked bank; do not invent a year. Missing years can be acceptable only with a demonstrably fair reviewed clue path.

Certified **Sampson** is the supported spelling; Jamel Sampson is documented. Accept Certified Samson as a search alias. Kees is an individual, not the band. Ro’dey's reported 30th-birthday event in 2018 requires conflict review against directory years. NALIS uses 1915 for Beryl McBurnie while other biographies use 1913; withhold exact year until resolved. Contemporary death/status notices demonstrate why mutable status should not be a permanent casual comparison.

The other 26 model years have public-source support, including some secondary biographies. None of these rows is an approved seed. Every graded claim, hint, major-career assignment and asset still needs import review.

## Folklore and data architecture

Keep folklore separate. Nominate Papa Bois, Mama D’leau, Soucouyant, Douen, La Diablesse and Lagahoo, then review Gang Gang Sarah and further additions. A folklore pack should use reviewed settings/forms/behaviours or a picture/clue ladder, with oral-tradition variants and aliases documented. Do not force people birth years/gender/schools onto spirits or score one local telling as universally correct.

Separate canonical identity, typed biographical facts, per-claim evidence, controlled vocabulary, editorial game profiles, asset permissions, clue ladders and immutable editions/history. People, folklore and Ting need distinct validated profiles. Design new vocabulary explicitly before imports; the legacy 69 options are not a limit on the owner's approved redesign. Migrate in a disposable environment before production, preserving existing identities/history.

## Next gate

1. Recognition check of the 32 nominations across target audience segments; replace weak nominees within caps.
2. Small unranked prototype and human playtests of four columns/hints; also try the no-Gender variant.
3. Approve rules, sources/unknown policy, roster and clue paths before schema/feature rewrite.
4. Implement one comparator, admin review, finite attempts, frozen editions, licensed content and regression checks in staging.

Human median 3–5 guesses, 90th percentile at most 7, and approximately 70% recognition in a small target pilot are provisional evaluation goals, not achieved results or population statistics. Expand gradually after a 32-person beta rather than padding it for more daily answers.
